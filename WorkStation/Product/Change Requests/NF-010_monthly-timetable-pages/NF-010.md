## 1. Request

> Monthly timetable per city ("Chennai prayer times November 2026") is a search IslamicFinder wins and SalahTime has no page for.

## 2. Goal & Acceptance Criteria

**Goal:** a crawlable monthly namaz timetable page per Indian city for the current and next month.

| # | Given / When / Then | Platform |
|---|---|---|
| AC-1 | `/prayer-times/india/chennai/november-2026` raw HTML has title "Chennai Prayer Times November 2026: Namaz Timetable", own canonical, H1, and a table with every day of the month: date, weekday, Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha | Web |
| AC-2 | In a browser the URL opens the Chennai page on that month (month table showing that month, today highlighted when inside it) | Web |
| AC-3 | Times equal the city page's month table for the same days (default settings) | Web |
| AC-4 | Pages exist for the **current and next month** of every Indian city; a past month URL redirects (301) to the city page; a month more than one ahead or a malformed month returns 404 | Web |
| AC-5 | The city page links "November 2026 timetable" and "December 2026 timetable"; each month page links the other month and the city page | Web |
| AC-6 | Month pages change only when the month changes (no daily re-upload) | Web |
| AC-7 | Android app unchanged | Android |

**Out of scope:** non-Indian cities (prerender computes IST only — `frontend/tools/prerender-seo.js:51`); PDF downloads; months beyond next.

## 4. Design Delta

**FDS**
- MON-01 URL `/prayer-times/<country>/<city>/<month>-<year>`, month in lowercase English (`november-2026`). Only current + next month exist; computed in IST at build time, so the nightly build of the 1st switches months.
- MON-02 Table: all days of the month; Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha (Karachi, Hanafi Asr, defaults); Fridays marked "Jumu'ah". Same calculation as the city page month table.
- MON-03 Title: "{City} Prayer Times {Month} {Year}: Namaz Timetable | SalahTime"; description: "{Month} {Year} namaz timetable for {City}, {State}: Fajr {first-day} to {last-day} …". No "today" in title or text (stable all month).
- MON-04 Canonical = the month URL; schema `WebPage` + `BreadcrumbList` (Prayer Times › City › Month); no FAQ.
- MON-05 Past month URLs 301 to the city page (server rule), so links and Google's old copies are not 404s.

**HLD:** city page route gains a month parameter; SEO tools gain a month builder and sitemap. **LLD:** None.

**Deviations from §3/§4 (approved design):**
- New `CityMonthComponent` instead of a month parameter on the city page: the city page's mobile layout (`AllPrayerTimesComponent`, what Google's smartphone crawler renders) has no month table, and the city page saves the visited city as the visitor's location. The new page shows the same table on every screen size, uses the default settings exactly like the prerender, and does not change the saved city.
- AC-4: the server can't tell a past month from a future one, so every unpublished `<month>-<year>` (past, two or more ahead, misspelt month) 301s to the city page; other junk (`/foo`) is a 404.
- MON-03 title falls back to "Chennai Prayer Times November 2026" when ": Namaz Timetable" makes it longer than 62 characters (most cities).
