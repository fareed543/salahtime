SalahTime - Current Namaz Timing, Namaz Time and Islamic Prayer Times

SalahTime helps Muslims check current namaz timing, daily prayer times and azan time from one simple web app. The app focuses on accurate Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha salah times, plus Sehri and Iftar timing for Ramadan. It is built for people who want a fast alternative to crowded prayer time sites while still keeping useful Islamic tools close at hand.

Prayer Times Today and Daily Prayer Time

Use SalahTime to view prayer times today for your selected location, save your salah calculation method, choose your madhab, and keep reminders for each prayer. The prayer timetable includes the main farz prayers, sunrise related timings, optional nafl windows and a daily salah calendar for planning worship throughout the month.

Islamic Prayer Times, Qibla Direction and Salah Calendar

SalahTime includes Qibla direction, duas, a tasbih counter, Ramadan calendar, Sehri time, Iftar time and zakat tools. Common searches such as namaz time, Islamic prayer times, Muslim salah times, what time is iftar today, Ramadan 2026 dates and azan time are supported through dedicated app sections and city prayer time pages where available.

Frequently Asked Salah Questions

Fajr prayer has 2 Sunnah rakat followed by 2 Fard rakat. Maghrib salah includes 3 Fard rakat, followed by 2 Sunnah and optional nafl prayers. Zuhr namaz commonly includes 4 Sunnah, 4 Fard, 2 Sunnah and optional nafl prayers. These summaries are included for quick reference while you use the app for daily salah times.

Browse supported city pages for prayer times by city, open the salah calendar for monthly Islamic prayer times, or use Qibla direction when preparing for prayer at home, work or while travelling.
