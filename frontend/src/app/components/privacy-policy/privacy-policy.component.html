Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SalahTime collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the SalahTime mobile application and related website features.

SalahTime is a prayer time and Islamic utility app that may use your device location, local device storage, notifications, and limited analytics tools in order to provide core app functionality.

Developer and Contact Information

If you have any questions, privacy requests, or complaints about this policy or the app, you can contact us at fareed543@gmail.com.

Information We Access or Collect

  • Location data: When you choose current-location features, the app accesses your device location to calculate prayer times, Qibla direction, nearby city selection, and related prayer reminders.
  • Search and settings data: The app stores selections such as city, calculation method, madhab, reminder settings, and onboarding preferences on your device.
  • Notification data: If you enable reminders, the app schedules local prayer notifications on your device.
  • Device and technical information: Limited device, browser, operating system, app interaction, and diagnostic information may be processed to improve app stability, performance, and usage measurement.

How We Use Information

  • To calculate and display accurate prayer times and Qibla-related features.
  • To remember your selected city and prayer settings.
  • To schedule and deliver local prayer reminders that you enable.
  • To improve app performance, reliability, and feature quality.
  • To measure general usage trends and maintain the service.

Location Data Handling

If you manually select a city, the app uses that city information for prayer time calculation. If you use current-location mode, the app may process your latitude and longitude on-device and may send location coordinates to our service endpoint to resolve a location name or nearest city.

Location access is optional, and you can deny permission and use a manually selected city instead.

Third-Party Services and Sharing

SalahTime may use third-party service providers that process limited data on our behalf for app functionality and measurement.

  • Google Analytics / Google tag services: Used to understand overall app and website usage trends.
  • Backend service endpoints: Used for features such as reverse geocoding and app update configuration.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with service providers only as needed to operate, secure, analyze, or improve the app, or when required by law.

Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Most user settings used by SalahTime are stored locally on your device. Cached location data and notification preferences may remain on the device until they expire, are overwritten, or you clear app data or uninstall the app.

Server-side logs, analytics records, and technical records may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, comply with legal obligations, and improve the service.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

  • You can deny or revoke location permission through your device settings.
  • You can disable prayer reminders and notifications from within the app or device settings.
  • You can switch from current location to manual city selection at any time.
  • You can clear locally stored app data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

SalahTime is not directed specifically to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date shown on this page. The latest version will remain available within the app and on the website.
