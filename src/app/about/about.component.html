About SalahTime

A visual representation of the five required daily prayers based on the sun's position.

The diagram illustrates the full 24-hour cycle (day and night). The key divisions are marked by celestial events that define the start of the five daily prayers:

FAJR: Pre-dawn prayer, starting when the sun is typically 18° below the horizon.
SUNRISE: Marks the end of Fajr time.
MIDDAY (ZOHR): Sun at its highest point (Solar Noon).
ASR: Afternoon prayer, based on shadow length.
MAGHRIB: Sunset.
ISHA: Night prayer, beginning when the sun is typically 18° below the horizon.
