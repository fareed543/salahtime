About SalahTime

A visual representation of the five required daily prayers based on the sun's position.

Salah timings follow the daily movement of the sun. Fajr begins at dawn when the first light spreads across the horizon and continues until the moment before sunrise. During sunrise itself, prayer is avoided. Once the sun passes its highest point in the sky, Dhuhr begins and lasts until late afternoon. Asr then starts when an object's shadow grows longer than its height and continues until sunset. Maghrib begins immediately after the sun fully disappears below the horizon and continues while the light in the sky gradually fades. When night darkness completely settles and twilight disappears, Isha begins and lasts until just before Fajr. These timings shift slightly every day depending on the season and your location, which is why accurate tracking tools like the SalahTime app are helpful.
